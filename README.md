## English

**Last Updated: August 27, 2026**

### 1. Privacy Summary

**SeeClear** (the “App”) is designed with privacy in mind. Photos, videos, recognized image content, and detailed analysis results are processed on your device and are not uploaded for analysis.

To improve the App and better serve our users, the App sends limited product-interaction analytics.

### 2. Photos, Camera, and Local Data

The App uses camera and photo-library access only for features you choose, such as color recognition, image analysis, and saving processed images. It accesses only the items you select or capture.

Analysis results and preferences are stored locally using Apple storage technologies. We cannot access this local data. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data.

### 3. Limited Analytics

We use **Aptabase** for privacy-focused analytics. We may record app launches, whether users choose recognition or analysis, whether a red-green or blue-yellow recognition session produced a result, which optional features are tried or completed, paywall and plan interactions, and purchase outcomes.

We do **not** send photos, videos, specific recognized colors, color-vision calibration answers or results, selected color-vision types, names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or free-form text.

Aptabase may receive basic technical information supplied by its SDK, such as a temporary session ID, app and system version, device model, and language/region. Its service temporarily processes IP address and User-Agent to generate coarse location and a daily-changing pseudonymous identifier. Analytics is processed in Aptabase’s European Union region and may be retained for up to five years. See the [Aptabase Privacy Policy](https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy).

### 4. Advertising, Tracking, and Accounts

The App contains no advertising, does not share data with advertising networks, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. No account or login is required.

### 5. Purchases

Apple processes all In-App Purchases. We do not receive your credit-card number or billing address. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock features and limited analytics such as plan category and purchase outcome.

### 6. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to **rtx3070757@gmail.com**.
